Academic Performance Audit & Strategic Remedial Plan

Audit Period: Academic Year 2025-2026 (Semesters 4-6)
Investigator: Samiksha Prashant Vartak
Total Sample: 250 Engineering Students
Status: Submitted for Faculty Action

1. Executive Summary

An academic audit was initiated to evaluate semester performance, subject-wise failure distributions, and the correlation between lecture attendance and examination outcomes. The analysis leveraged data cleaned via SQL queries and transformed into an interactive Power BI dashboard.

2. Low-Performance Areas Identified

  • Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA): Lowest cohort average (58.32%) with 30 failing students (12.0% failure rate). Major bottlenecks observed in dynamic programming and tree traversals.
  • Operating Systems (OS): Second lowest average (60.20%) with 30 failing students (12.0% failure rate), primarily driven by memory management and semaphore synchronization concepts.
  • Database Management Systems (DBMS): 8.0% failure rate with strong query performance but weak relational schema normalizations.

3. Attendance Shortfall Impact

A stark divergence in academic performance is directly tied to the mandatory 75% attendance threshold:

Attendance Cohort Student Count Average Score % Failures Failure Rate
Attendance Defaulters (< 75%) 49 Students 47.18% 42 Students 85.71%
Regular Attendance (>= 75%) 201 Students 67.71% 24 Students 11.94%

4. Recommended Remedial Interventions

  1. Mandatory Remedial Bridge Sessions: Conduct 2 hours of weekly problem-solving clinics for DSA and OS for the 42 identified High-Risk students.
  2. Parental Counseling & Bi-Weekly Attendance Notices: Issue formal notices to all 49 students falling under the 75% attendance criteria.
  3. Continuous Formative Assessments: Transition from 100% summative end-sem tests to bi-weekly 10-minute quizzes to catch learning gaps early.
